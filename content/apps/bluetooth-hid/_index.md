Bluetooth HID turns your Android phone into a **Bluetooth keyboard, mouse and presentation
remote** for any computer. There is nothing to install on the computer: it pairs like a normal
Bluetooth keyboard. It's free and open source:
[github.com/nosari20/Bluetooth-HID](https://github.com/nosari20/Bluetooth-HID).

### Scan and type

Scan a QR code or barcode and type its content on your computer: Wi-Fi keys, long passwords,
URLs, serial numbers, configuration strings. You see the text before it's sent, and characters
that the computer's keyboard layout can't type are highlighted. A progress bar, *Cancel* and
*Resume* (if the connection drops) keep long texts under control.

### Exact typing, every time

The computer's keyboard layout decides which character each key prints, so the app types for
the layout you choose and remembers it per computer: **US QWERTY** and **French AZERTY**
(Windows, Linux and Mac variants). Accented letters (é, è, à, ç, ê, ï…) are supported. Repeated
characters and Shift/AltGr changes are handled, and keys are never left stuck down.

### Keyboard

Type with your usual Android keyboard (swipe and voice typing work) and the text appears live on
the computer, or write a block of text and send it in one go. A special-keys bar gives you Esc,
Tab, arrows, Home/End, Page Up/Down, F1–F12, sticky Ctrl/Shift/Alt/AltGr/Win (⌘), common
shortcuts and media keys.

### Mouse

A touchpad with pointer acceleration: tap to click, two-finger tap for right-click, two-finger
scroll, long-press to drag, and hold-to-drag buttons.

### Presenter

Big *Next* and *Previous* areas for PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides and PDF viewers. The
phone's **volume keys change slides** too, with a short vibration on each change. You can play
videos embedded in PowerPoint, and lock the screen YouTube-style so that only the volume keys
work during your talk.

### Private by design

No internet permission, no ads, no analytics. Your text goes only to the computer you
connected. See the [privacy policy](/apps/bluetooth-hid/privacy/).

### Requirements

Android 16 or newer with Bluetooth HID device support (Pixel phones and most recent devices),
and a Windows, macOS or Linux computer with Bluetooth.

*Tip:* if typing does nothing after using another keyboard app on the same computer, remove the
phone from the computer's Bluetooth devices and pair again.
