MaClasse is an Android app for teachers in French *collèges* and *lycées*. It keeps your
classes, pupils, timetable, seating plans, groups and reminders on your phone, with no account
and no server.

The optional **MaClasse Pro** yearly subscription adds Pronote timetable sync and Pronote
student-list import.
